# DocuReady Shop

High-converting, modern e-commerce storefront for **DocuReady Shop** built with Vite, React 18, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.

Zero proprietary lock-in. 100% portable and ready to deploy anywhere (Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, VPS).

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Visual Identity & Design**: Clean slate-dark navigation, vibrant purple gradients, liquid glowing blobs, and frosted glass cards matching the official brand guidelines.
* **Focused 3-Pillar Catalog**:
  1. **Graphic Design & Branding** (Logo Suites, Social Creative Packs)
  2. **Web Design & Development** (Business Landing Pages, Multi-Page Portals, Online Catalogs)
  3. **App Development** (Android Mobile Apps & Universal Web Apps)
* **App Tiers & Clear Distinctions**:
  * **Android Mobile App**: Standard at **₹3,599** (Signed APK, 4 core screens) & Premium at **₹8,999** (Play Store bundle, live database, authentication & Razorpay).
  * **Universal Web App (PWA)**: Standard at **₹3,599** (Instant link, PWA home-screen installable) & Premium at **₹8,999** (Full-stack cloud web app with database, auth & payments).
  * In-depth comparison matrix for clients to easily choose the right package.
* **Streamlined Cart & WhatsApp Checkout Workflow**:
  * Persistent multi-item cart.
  * Direct checkout with unique Order Reference generation (`#DR-1041`).
  * Instant pre-formatted WhatsApp dispatcher to `+91 90048 04043` for fast confirmation.
* **Self-Service Order Tracking**:
  * Client dashboard at `/dashboard` where clients enter their email to see live statuses (`Pending`, `Confirmed`, `In Progress`, `Completed`).
* **Admin Operations Control Center**:
  * Access at `/admin` (Default passkey: `admin123` or `ashleyghose64@gmail.com`).
  * Live revenue, pending, production, and completed order metrics.
  * Order status management pipeline with direct 1-click WhatsApp messaging to clients.
  * Discounts & Promo Manager (Global % banner and package-level discounts).
  * Custom Services Manager (Add new custom packages or hide built-ins).
  * Payment & WhatsApp Phone Configuration.
* **Full Legal Suite**:
  * `/terms` — Terms & Conditions
  * `/privacy` — Privacy Policy
  * `/refund` — Refund & Cancellation Policy
